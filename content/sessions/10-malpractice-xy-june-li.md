Malpractice is a Vienna-based artist duo composed of Chiara Kristler (b. 1998, IT/AT) and Marcin Ratajczyk (b. 2002, PL). Their practice constructs functional systems to examine how human communication and societal structures adapt to algorithmic logic. Much of their work runs on a method they call prompted perspectives: a language model is given a worldview and made to run it continuously against live material, so that a way of seeing becomes something an audience can enter, test and leave.

Their works include *Flynn* (2025–ongoing), an AI agent admitted as a student to the University of Applied Arts Vienna; the interactive performance *All Inclusive AI Hell Cruise* (2026); the film *Fatigue Archive* (2025); and the AI opera *Don't Be Such An Image* (2025). Malpractice held a solo exhibition at Francisco Carolinum, Linz (2025–26), were nominated for the S+T+ARTS Prize 2026 and are Rauschenberg Residents at ZKM Karlsruhe (2026–27).

Xy June Li is a research-based interdisciplinary artist and writer based in Stuttgart. Her practice spans video installation, performance, painting and text. She studied literature in Tübingen and graduated from the Academy of Fine Arts Stuttgart in 2024. Her long-term project *Kuling* (since 2023) takes the 1895 British colonial resort on Mount Lu, near where she grew up, as a field for asking how language carries violence and how fiction can intervene in historical fact. *MOAT (Ministry of Artificial Truth)*, still in an early stage, continues this question into AI: a fictional ministry issues prompts to large language models to expose the authoritarian registers latent in them. She is an Academic Associate on Critical Faculties at HfG Karlsruhe.
